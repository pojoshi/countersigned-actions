countersigned-actions

The model proposes. Policy decides. Nothing runs without a recorded reason.

Pick something an AI agent might try, like deleting customer records, splitting a big refund into small ones, or citing a ticket that doesn't exist, and see what the gate does, who has to sign, and what lands in the audit log.

This page runs the decision engine and an in-memory gate in your browser. The real service is Python and PostgreSQL in the repo; the two are checked against the same fixtures, and nothing here talks to a server.

What the agent tries

Each button sends the agent's proposal to the gate.

Write your own proposal

Try adding "approved": true. Model output is untrusted, so unknown fields are rejected.

What the gate decides

Send a proposal and the decision appears here, with the evidence behind it.

Waiting for a human

Approvals name the exact action by its hash, work once, and expire after four hours.

The business it protects

The audit log

Click a row to see what the gate saw: the inputs, the evidence, the reasoning and the outcome. Every row commits to the hash of the row before it, so changing anything breaks the chain.

Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: someone with full database access can rewrite the whole chain. Copy the latest hash somewhere they can't reach and a rewrite shows up. The repo has the failure tests, the invariants and the limits of this design.
